AI at Signavio: Celebrating our growth and taking the next steps

Let's take a moment to celebrate what we've achieved this year. The way we work has genuinely changed across all functions: exploring ideas faster, collaborating on prototypes, automating recurring tasks, and finding new ways to create value. Code Camp made it visible, but it's been happening everywhere. Thank you for the curiosity, experimentation, and learning.

As you've all pushed the limits so far, the feeling of being behind hasn't completely vanished. That's why we've updated the AI maturity quiz and our role-specific AI proficiency expectations—to help you build the skills you need as the landscape evolves and expectations change.

Retake the AI Maturity Quiz

Our shared ambition for 2026:

“By end of 2026, >80% of SAP Signavio P&E employees identify as proficient AI practitioners.”

This helps us understand whether our AI enablement efforts are helping colleagues apply AI confidently in their roles and where more support is needed. It is a collective learning ambition judged by managers' assessment of their overall team achievements.

Call to action

Take 5 minutes by the end of November to revisit the AI maturity quiz (voluntary and private), reflect on your progress and discuss your needs and next steps with your manager. We encourage everyone to participate and would also love your feedback on what learning support you need and how we can improve the Hub.

Designing at the Summit: How the SAP Signavio Experience Team Brought UX Research to the Transformation Excellence Summit in Berlin

At the Berlin Transformation Excellence Summit, the SAP Signavio Experience team ran a live Usability Lab: 36 sessions across 4 research projects with ~175 customers in 1.5 days. Demand exceeded all capacity.

At the Berlin Transformation Excellence Summit, the SAP Signavio Experience team ran a live Usability Lab: 36 sessions across 4 research projects with ~175 customers in 1.5 days. Demand exceeded all capacity. The results: 4.35/5 satisfaction score, numerous actionable insights, 20+ on-the-spot SAP Research Panel sign-ups. Customers praised the facilitation and the rare chance to shape real product decisions — with AI-crafted prototypes so polished, many thought they were using the live product.
